## Title

RESHOOT

## Short description (tagline)

You're the star of a movie that changes genre every five minutes — and the director won't stop yelling "CUT!".

## Description

**RESHOOT** is a solo arena FPS where you play the lead actor of a film whose director keeps rewriting the rules mid-fight.

Knights, drones, gangsters: the extras come at you while a tyrannical director freezes the action, shouts his whims and grades your performance live on the **audience meter**. Every death is just a failed take — the scene starts again from the clap. No game over, only retakes.

**Three movies, one set**
- **Medieval** — sword, shield and bow against charging knights and archers. Watch out for the wooden horse convoy.
- **Space** — zero-gravity combat with a laser rifle against drones, until the artificial gravity kicks back in.
- **Film noir** — a six-shot pistol in a rain-soaked alley, black and white. Gangsters duck behind cover, peek out to fire bursts and run for new cover when you get close. Then the power goes out, and only the muzzle flashes light the street.
- **Director's Cut finale** — he can't choose, so he keeps everything: the genre switches every 20 seconds.

**Rehearsal, then the real thing**
Every scene starts on a cheap cardboard set. At the end of the first take, the director calls for better staging: a curtain of light sweeps across the stage and swaps every prop for its realistic version, right in front of you.

**"CUT!" — Directives**
Mid-take, everything freezes and the director imposes a rule: headshots only, keep moving, no reloading ("no cuts!"), no shield, close combat... Follow it to boost your ratings.

**Controls** (keyboard and mouse)
| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| WASD / ZQSD | Move (in space: move where you look) |
| Mouse | Aim |
| Shift | Sprint (in space: boost) |
| Space | Jump (in space: go up) |
| Ctrl / C | Crouch, slide while sprinting (in space: go down) |
| Left click | Fire / attack |
| Right click | Aim / shield |
| R | Reload |
